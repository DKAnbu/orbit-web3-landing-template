FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to own a GPU to use ORBIT?

No. Renters only need a wallet and ORB (or any supported stablecoin, converted on the fly). Owning a GPU is only required if you want to earn as a provider.

How are providers verified?

Each node runs an attestation check that proves its hardware and software stack, recorded on-chain. Nodes that fail attestation or go offline during a job are slashed.

What happens if a job fails mid-run?

The job is automatically re-routed to another node at no extra cost, and the failed provider's stake is partially slashed to compensate the renter.

Is there a minimum stake?

Staking starts at 100 ORB. Governance voting requires at least 1,000 ORB at snapshot time.

Where can I buy ORB?

ORB trades on major decentralized exchanges on Ethereum and Base. Always verify the contract address shown at the top of this page.
